## Settings documented in the assembly tutorial

- Printer: Bambu Lab A1 Mini.
- Wall loops: 3.
- Support: enabled, using Tree support.
- Orientation: follow the slicer screenshot in Step 1 of the assembly tutorial.

The supplied text does not specify material, layer height, infill, temperatures, or a slicer profile. STL files do not encode units; confirm the intended dimensions in the slicer before printing. These files alone do not establish competition compliance.

Remove all support material and clean the mounting holes, slots, and mating surfaces before assembly. Check the motor fit and mounting hardware before tightening screws. Avoid overtightening screws into printed parts.

The metal or copper base plate, front shield, and blade are separate components; their fabrication drawings are not included in this folder. Weigh and measure the completed robot, including the battery and all hardware, against the applicable competition rules.
